## Nab'ey ximb'äl

1. **Tatikirisaj rik'in ri ximonel** — Xtak'ül jun ximonel ewanin. Tatikirisaj chuqa' taya' jun ewan retal e b'i'aj.
2. **Taya' retal awachib'al** — Taya' PIN, taya' retal samajib'äl, chuqa' taya' k'oje' ri kilïb' ewanum.
3. **Tatikirisaj** — Wakami xat ütz chik richin nawokisaj Llamenos.

## Ruk'ijlem 5 ramaj

### 1. Tatz'eta' ri rupas

We xtatikirisaj, xtaq'axaj ri nab'ey ruwäch chuqa' ri rupas.

### 2. Taya' retal awachib'al

Tachapa' ri awachib'al. Wakami yetikïr naya' retal awachib'al.

### 3. Rucholajel ajchajinel

We at ajchajinel, yetikïr naya' retal taq k'axk'olil, taq samaj, taq riqom.

### 4. Rucholajel to'onel

We at to'onel, yetikïr nak'axaj taq riqom, tatz'ib'aj taq tzijol, takamuluj taq axan.

### 5. Rucholajel chajinel

We at chajinel, yetikïr natäq jun rutzijol, takamuluj ri taq xtaq.

## Achike ruk'axk'olil samajib'äl?

Llamenos nisamajïk pa konojel taq k'ak' raqän. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.
