## Tikatirisaj pa ruk'ojlem ajchajinel

We xtatikirisaj nab'ey, Llamenos nuya' chi awe ye'ek'atzin taq wachib'al.

### Sihirbaza mîhengan

1. Tacha' **ch'ab'äl chuqa' k'ojlemal**.
2. Taya' **rub'i' chuqa' retal ruchakub'al**.
3. **Tajaq taq b'ey**:
   - Twilio / SignalWire / Vonage / Plivo
   - WhatsApp Business API
   - Signal Bridge
4. Taya' **taq molaj k'ojlemal**.
5. Taya' **taq samaj k'ojlemal**.

## Ruk'ulb'al winaq

### Ruk'axik winaq

1. B'e pa **Users > Invite**.
2. Tatz'ib'aj ri taq taqojb'al e-mail.
3. Tacha' **ruk'ojlem** (Admin, Volunteer, Reporter).
4. Tacha' **Send Invites**.

Ri winaq xtak'ül jun ximonel ewanin chuqa' xruk'oje' ri rub'i'aj. Man k'atzinel ta chi ye'ak'ül ewanum o ko'tz'ib'.

### Taq ruk'ojlem chuqa' ya'oj q'ij

| Ruk'ojlem | Achike nib'an |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Admin** | Konojel: ruk'olib'al, winaq, kamulaxik, kib'anatajib'al |
| **Volunteer** | Riqom, tzijol, tzijol, taq rutzijol eto'on |
| **Reporter** | Xa xe rutaqikisaxik chuqa' rukamulaxik taq rutzijol |

### Tutz'apij b'i'aj

We nawajo' chi nayüj jun winaq o chi nuch'utinisaj ri ya'oj q'ij:

1. Tachapa' pa rub'i'.
2. Tacha' **Change Role** o **Deactivate Account**.
3. Taya' retal ri jaloj.

> **Rutzijol retal:** Tacha' Deactivate, man ta Delete — richin nik'oje' el ri ruk'amulaxik.

## Taq samaj chuqa' ruk'ojlem

### ¿Achike ri taq samaj?

Ri taq samaj nikiya' retal ri ramaj ri yeto'on richin yejak' taq riqom. Llamenos nuyäk taq riqom chi ke ri e k'o pa samaj.

### ¿Achike ninyinäq jun samaj?

1. B'e pa **Shifts > New Shift**.
2. Taya' **rub'i', ramaj, chuqa' rujaloj**.
3. Taya' **molaj / to'onel**.
4. Tacha' **Save**.

## Ruchajixik ri q'inoj

### Rupas

Wakami yetikïr nataq'axaj:

- Achike to'onel e k'axel
- Achike taq winaq e k'o pa rucholajem
- Ri taq tzijol chuqa' taq rutzijol e jaqom
- Taq jaloj pa taq samaj

### Ruwäch riqom

Konojel riqom, tzijol, chuqa' tzijol e tz'ib'anïk. Yetikïr nakanoj chi rij q'ij, b'i'aj, o to'onel.

### Audit Log

Konojel samaj e tz'ib'anïk pa ronojel ruk'ojlemal. We nawajo' nawetamaj achike rub'eyal, b'e pa **Audit Log**.

## Taq kib'anatajib'al ewanum

- **2FA / Passkey** — Chilab'enïk chi re jun ajchajinel.
- **Session Timeout** — Richin nuchajij taq samajib'äl e majun taq winaq.
- **Export Settings** — Richin nik'oje' el o nikopïx taq kib'anatajib'al.

## Ruk'ijlem chuwäch chi ye'ek'atzin

- Konojel to'onel xuk'ül ri ruk'axik
- Taq samaj pa taq q'ij e k'oje'
- Taq b'ey taq tzijol ütz nikib'äx
- Konojel wetamaj toq yek'ax pa ri ajchajinel
