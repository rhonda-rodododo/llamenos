## Nab'ey ximb'äl: Taya' jun b'i'aj

We xak'ül ri ruk'axik, xkatq'axaj jun ximonel ewanin.

1. **Tatikirisaj ri ximonel** chuqa' taya' jun ewan PIN.
2. **Taya' k'oje' ri kilïb' ewanum** — re' xa xe richin taq q'ijub'.
3. **Tatikirisaj** chuqa' xkatkol pa ri rupas rech Chajinel.

## Tatz'ib'aj jun rutzijol k'ak'a'

1. Tachapa' **New Report**.
2. **Taya' rub'i'** — re' ri yetz'et ruma konojel (richin niya' retal).
3. **Tatz'ib'aj rupam** — re' **ewanin chuwäch chi nik'ëx ri k'ak' raqän**.
4. We k'o, **taya' taq ruwi'** — junjun ewanin.
5. Tachapa' **Submit**.

> **Nab'ey retal:** Xa xe rat chuqa' ri taq winaq eto'on yetikïr nikisik'ij ri awachib'al ewanin. Ri ruk'waxik xa xe nusik'ij ciphertext.

## Takamuluj ri awachib'al

We xtaq ri rutzijol, yetikïr nataq'axaj pa **My Reports** achike rub'eyal:

- **Submitted** — K'a yek'owisaj.
- **In Review** — K'o jun xusach ruq'ij.
- **Resolved** — Xsolïx.

We jun ajchajinel xtuya' retal, yetikïr nasik'ij chi rij ri rutzijol. Ri retal chuqa' ewanin.

## Taya' retal awachib'al

Pa **Settings** yetikïr naya' retal:

- Rub'i' taqojb'al chuqa' rub'i' pa ruwäch
- PIN o biometric
- Ruk'ijlem b'i'aj
- Taq samajib'äl e jaqom

## We man intikïr ta nik'öx

- **Xinmes ruwa PIN** — Tachapa' **Forgot PIN** chuqa' tikirel taq ximonel.
- **Xtz'apij ri b'i'aj** — Taya' retal chi re ri ajchajinel.
- **K'ak'a' samajib'äl** — K'atzinel chi nawokisaj jun k'ak'a' samajib'äl. Pa ri ruxaq nik'öx tacha' **Add Device**.
