## K'ak' Telefono rik'in K'ïy Taq Rucha'ojil

**5 taq rucha'ojil oqom** — Tacha' chi rij Twilio, SignalWire, Vonage, Plivo o Asterisk e ruyon. Taya' rutzub'al awachib'al ri rucha'ojil pa ri kib'anatajib'al ruk'waxik o toq yek'ulun taq ruk'olib'al. Tajal ri rucha'ojil chi jas ri k'o ak'u'x xab'akuchi' majun jaloj riqom.

**WebRTC ruk'olib'al pa k'ak' raqän** — Ri taq to'onel yetikïr nikib'äx pa k'ak' raqän riqom majun ochochib'äl. Niya' k'ak' token WebRTC chi kijujunal taq rucha'ojil chi re Twilio, SignalWire, Vonage chuqa' Plivo. Tiya' rutzub'al awachib'al pa ronojel to'onel (ochochib'äl, k'ak' raqän o keb'ala').

## Ruk'ulb'al taq Riqom

**Sib'alaj chi rij ri taq tzij** — Toq jun winaq nrïl, konojel ri taq to'onel e k'o pa samaj majun e sachinïk yetikïr nikib'äx junam riqom. Ri nab'ey xtïkïr nrokisaj ri q'ij; ri ch'aqa' chik taq tzij yek'owisaj rïnïk.

**Ruk'olib'al chi rij ri taq samaj** — Taya' rutzub'al awachib'al ri taq samaj pa ri k'ak' taq q'ij rik'in ruk'ijlem jalajïk. Taya' ri taq to'onel pa ri taq samaj. Ri q'inoj yetikïr nruk'ül taq riqom chi ke ri e k'o pa samaj.

**Rucholajem tzij rik'in ruq'ojom tzij** — We konojel ri taq to'onel e sachinïk, ri taq winaq yetikïr yekik'owisaj jun rucholajem tzij rik'in ruq'ojom tzij. Ruk'ijlem rucholajem tzij ya'on rutzub'al awachib'al (30-300 taq ch'utiramaj). We majun nrokisaj, ri taq riqom yetikïr yekïl rik'in ochochib'äl.

**Ochochib'äl tzij ruk'olb'al** — Ri taq winaq yetikïr nikitz'ib'aj jun rutzijol ewanum (k'a 5 taq ch'utiramaj) we majun to'onel nrokisaj. Ri ochochib'äl taq tzijol yetikïr nikitz'ib'aj ruma Whisper AI chuqa' ewanin chi re ri ajchajinel.

## Taq Tzijol Ewanin

**Tz'ib'anïk taq tzijol rik'in ewanum** — Ri taq to'onel yetikïr nikitz'ib'aj taq tzijol toq ek'o ri q'ij chuqa' chi rij ri q'ij. Ri taq tzijol ewanin pa ri ruk'ojlem to'onel rik'in ECIES (secp256k1 + XChaCha20-Poly1305) chuwäch chi nik'ëx ri k'ak' raqän. Ri ruk'waxik ruma q'inoj xa xe yerusamajij ri ciphertext.

**Ewanum keb'ïk** — Jun tzijol jun ewanum keb'ïk: jun chi re ri to'onel xutz'ib'aj, jun chik chi re ri ajchajinel. Ri keb'ala' yetikïr nikijïk chi kijujunal. Majun chik winaq yetikïr nisik'ij ri rupam.

**Taq ruk'ojlem ichinil** — Ri taq ajchajinel nikiya' ruk'ojlem ichinil chi re ri taq tzijol: tz'ib', ajilab'äl, rucha'ojil, retal, ruk'ojlem tz'ib'. Ri taq ruk'ojlem ewanin rik'in ri rupam tzijol.

**Ruyon rutz'aqikisaxik taq b'ano'** — Ri taq tzijol ruyon yerutz'aqikisaj pa k'ak' raqän achi'el taq b'ano' ewanin. We nitzolinïk ri ruxaq o we xel ri to'onel, ri samaj xb'an yek'oje' el. Ri taq b'ano' ye'el toq xtzeqelb'ëx ruk'ojlem.

## Rutz'ib'axik ruma AI

**Rutz'ib'axik pa ri samajib'äl** — Ri taq riqom yetikïr nikitz'ib'äx ruma AI ri nisamajïk pa k'ak' raqän. Ri oqom majun q'ij nok pa ja. Xa xe ri rutz'ib'axik ewanin yek'oje' el.

**Taq kichajixik ajchajinel chuqa' to'onel** — Ri taq ajchajinel yetikir nikichajij ri rutz'ib'axik chi junamalik. Ri taq to'onel yetikïr nikich'äk chi kijujunal. Keb'ala' k'o junan kich'aweb'al.

**Taq rutz'ib'axik ewanin** — Ri taq rutz'ib'axik nikokisaj ri junan ewanum ECIES achi'el ri taq tzijol. Ri rutz'ib'axik yerusamajij xa xe ri ciphertext.

## Ruch'utinisaxik Spam

**Voice CAPTCHA** — Jalajïk richin niya' retal bot oqom: ri winaq nïl yetikïr nab'ey jun ketz'ïb' 4 ajilab'äl chuqa' k'o chi tuya' pa ri keypad. Nuch'upij ri oqom pa ruyon, xa xe chi re ri k'ak' taq winaq yetikïr nikib'äx.

**Ruch'utinisaxik ruk'ijlem** — Ruch'utinisaxik ruk'ijlem chi kijujunal taq ochochib'äl, ek'oje'el pa ri q'inoj. Ri taq ruch'utinisaxik ya'on rutzub'al awachib'al e k'oje' chi rij ri nitzolinïk.

**Taq rucholajem k'axk'olil pa ri ramaj** — Ri taq ajchajinel nikichajij taq rucholajem k'axk'olil ochochib'äl rik'in jun o pa k'ïy. Ri taq k'axk'olil e k'oje' rïnïk. Ri taq winaq e k'axk'olil yetikïr nikib'äx jun rutzijol q'atexem.

**Taq rutaqoj ICR ichinil** — Taya' retal taq rutaqoj oqom chi kijujunal taq ch'ab'äl. Ri q'inoj nikokisaj ri aweta'mab'al chi re ri taq b'ey ICR, xa xe chi majun k'o toq xtiya' rutzub'al awachib'al rik'in oqom pa ruyon.

## Rutaqoj Tzij pa K'ïy Taq B'ey

**SMS** — Rutaqoj tzij pa kinäq chuqa' pa retal rik'in Twilio, SignalWire, Vonage o Plivo. Tzolinïk rutzijol k'a ri' toq niya' retal jun winaq. Ri taq tzijol yetikïr yek'oje' el pa jun rutzub'al awachib'al axan.

**WhatsApp Business** — Tach'akunïk rik'in Meta Cloud API (Graph API v21.0). K'o richin yeruya' kitzijol taq wachib'al richin nikichap taq axan pa 24 ramaj. K'o chilab'enïk pa taq wachib'al, taq wuj chuqa' oqom.

**Signal** — Rutaqoj tzij rik'in ruchajixik pa k'ak' raqän rik'in jun rupam signal-cli-rest-api. Ruchajixik chuqa' rutz'aqikisaxik e ub'antajik. Rutz'ib'axik taq rutzijol oqom rik'in Whisper AI pa ri samajib'äl.

**Taq axan e b'ano'** — Konojel taq b'ey taq tzijol yetikïr yek'oje' el pa jun rutzub'al awachib'al axan. B'ala' taq tzijol rik'in ruk'ijlem chuqa' ruk'ojlem b'ey. K'exoj pa ri ramaj. Konojel taq tzijol ewanin pa ri awuk'waxik toq ye'apon. Ri ruk'waxik xa xe yerusamajij ri ciphertext.

## Taq Tzijol Ewanin

**Ruk'ojlem chajinel** — Jun ruk'ojlem ruk' chajinel chi re ri winaq nikitaq kitzijol chuqa' nikitz'ib'aj taq rutzijol. Ri taq chajinel xa xe nikil pa jun rutzub'al awachib'al jeb'ël rik'in taq rutzijol chuqa' to'onel. E k'axk'olil chi ke rik'in ri junam b'ey achi'el ri taq to'onel, rik'in jun rucha'ojil ruk'ojlem.

**Taq rutzijol ewanin** — Ri rupam tzijol ewanin rik'in ECIES chuwäch chi nik'ëx ri k'ak' raqän. Ri taq b'i'aj tz'ib'anïk pa retal richin niya' retal, ri rupam ewanin richin ruchajixik. Ri taq ruwi' ch'utiwuj ewanin chi kijujunal.

**Rub'eyal samaj tzijol** — Taq rucha'ojil richin niya' retal ri taq tzijol. Ruk'ojlem rub'eyal (jaqom, xch'akub'äx, xsolïx). Ri taq ajchajinel yetikïr nikich'akub'aj taq tzijol chuqa' nikitzolinïk rik'in taq rutzijol ewanin.

## Rucholajel Tz'ib'

**Taq ruwi' tz'ib' ewanin** — Taya' k'oje' ri taq rutzijol tz'ib' rik'in ewanum chi rij ri b'ey. Ri taq b'i'aj, ochochib'äl, taq taqojb'al chuqa' taq tzijol ewanin chuwäch chi nik'ëx ri k'ak' raqän.

**Ruchajixik taq achi'elal** — Tach'akunisaj ri taq tz'ib' chi rij e pa kibi', chuqa' rik'in taq riqom, taq axan chuqa' taq tzijol. Taya' jun wachib'al chi kijujunal chi re ri winaq yato'on.

**Ruyon ruch'akunisaxik** — Ri taq riqom chuqa' taq tzijol e k'oje' jun ruch'akunisaxik rik'in ri taq tz'ib' etaman kib' ruma ri nikïl taq ochochib'äl.

**Ruk'ulb'al richin taq molaj** — Tachajij achike taq winaq e k'o pa molaj yetikïr nikil achike taq tz'ib'. Ri taq ya'oj q'ij e k'o pa k'ojlemal chuqa' yetikir niya' rutzub'al awachib'al.

**Taq retal chuqa' ruk'ulb'al** — Taya' retal ri taq tz'ib' rik'in taq retal. Ri taq rub'eyal ruk'ulb'al nuk'ül ri k'ak' taq tz'ib' chi re ri nik'aslem.

**Ruyakb'al chuqa' ruq'atexem** — Taya' k'ak' taq tz'ib' pa ri CSV o JSON. Taya' retal taq b'ano' ewanin. Konojel ri samaj nisamajïk pa ri awak'ak' raqän.

## Taq Ya'oj Q'ij E K'oje' pa K'ojlemal

**Taq ruk'ojlem ichinil** — Taya' retal awinaqil taq ruk'ojlem rik'in ri ya'oj q'ij yek'atzin tiqo. Tatikirisaj rik'in ri taq wachib'al (Ajchajinel, To'onel, Chajinel) o tatikirisaj pa k'a ri q'ij.

**Taq ya'oj q'ij pa k'ojlemal** — 90+ ya'oj q'ij ichinil pa 17 taq ruk'ojlem. Tachajij achike winaq yetikïr nitz'et, niya', nujäl chuqa' nuyüj pa jun jeb'ël ruk'ojlemal.

**Ruch'akunisaxik molaj** — Taya' ri taq winaq e k'o pa molaj pa taq molaj. Ri taq ya'oj q'ij yetikïr yek'oje' el pa jujun taq molaj, ke re' ke ri jalan taq molaj e k'ojlemal taq tzijol e jalan.

## Rupas Ajchajinel

**Ruk'olib'al ruma ajtz'ib'** — Ruk'olib'al k'ïy taq q'ij toq nab'ey xtik'owisaj ri ajchajinel. Tacha' achike taq b'ey yejaq (Oqom, SMS, WhatsApp, Signal, Tzijol), taya' rutzub'al awachib'al ri taq rucha'ojil chuqa' taya' rub'i' awoktaq'ij.

**Rucholajem k'oxom tatikirisaj** — Widget pa ri rupas richin niya' retal ri ruk'ijlem ruk'olib'al: rutz'ub'al awachib'al taq b'ey, ruk'ulb'al taq to'onel, ruyak'ik taq samaj.

**Ruchajixik pa ri ramaj** — Tatz'eta' ri taq riqom e k'o pa samaj, ri taq winaq e k'o pa rucholajem, taq axan chuqa' rub'eyal taq to'onel pa ri ramaj. Ri taq rutz'aqikisaxik yek'owisaj rïnïk.

**Ruk'ulb'al winaq** — Taya' k'axk'olil ri k'ak' taq winaq e k'o pa molaj rik'in taq ojqan taq ximonel. E yetikir nikiya' ri kib'anatajib'al chuqa' ri taq ewanum. Taya' retal ri taq ruk'ojlem, ya'oj q'ij chuqa' ruk'ojlem molaj.

**Rutz'ib'axik Audit** — Junanïk ri q'ij, tzijol, taq rutzijol, taq rutzijol tzijol, taq jaloj kib'anatajib'al chuqa' taq samaj ajchajinel e tz'ib'anïk. Rutz'ub'al awachib'al pa k'ïy taq ruxaq richin ri taq ajchajinel.

**Ruwäch riqom** — Ruwäch riqom ri yetikïr nikikanoj chuqa' nikich'ob' ruma ruk'ijlem q'ij, rukanoj ochochib'äl chuqa' ruk'ojlem to'onel. Rutz'aqikisaxik tzijol chi rij GDPR.

**To'onel pa ri samaj** — Taq rutzijol taq k'utunïk, taq rucholajel taq ruch'ob'anel, taq retal samaj richin taq ruk'aqatsb'al chuqa' ruchajixik. Yetikïr yek'il pa ri ruch'akub'al raqän chuqa' pa ri rucholajem taq b'ey.

## Rutzub'al awachib'al To'onel

**Rucholajem taq b'ey** — Tapitz' Ctrl+K (o CmdK pa Mac) richin yalan aninäq rutikirisaxik, rukanoj, ruyak'ik tzijol chuqa' rujaloj rutzub'al awachib'al. Ri taq b'ey xa xe richin ajchajinel ye'ik'o pa ruk'ojlem ruk'ojlem.

**Taq rutzijol k'ij** — Ri taq riqom yetikïr nikiya' retal oqom pa k'ak' raqän, rutzijol push chuqa' rutz'aqikisaxik b'i'aj pa ri ruxaq. Tacha' junanïk ri rucha'ojil rutzijol pa ri kib'anatajib'al.

**K'oje' to'onel** — Ri taq ajchajinel nikil ri ramaj ri e k'o pa k'amaya'l, e majun chik chuqa' e pa q'ijub'. Ri taq to'onel yetikïr nikich'äk ri switch pa q'ijub' pa ri ruch'akub'al raqän richin nikichupij ri taq riqom majun e majun chik chuwäch chi nekejach ri samaj.

**Taq ruk'aqatsb'al pa keypad** — Tapitz' ? richin nitz'et konojel ri taq ruk'aqatsb'al e k'o. Tach'akunisaj pa taq ruxaq, tajaq ri rucholajem taq b'ey chuqa' tab'ana' taq samaj majan majun ochochib'äl.

**Ruyon rutz'aqikisaxik taq b'ano' tzijol** — Ri taq tzijol ruyon yerutz'aqikisaj pa k'ak' raqän achi'el taq b'ano' ewanin. We nitzolinïk ri ruxaq o we xel ri to'onel, ri samaj xb'an yek'oje' el. Ri taq b'ano' ye'el toq xtzeqelb'ëx ruk'ojlem.

**Rutz'aqikisaxik tzijol ewanin** — Taya' retal ri taq tzijol achi'el jun ruyak'bal tzijol ewanin (.enc) ri nuk'äm ri' rik'in GDPR, ewanin ruma ri awanum ruk'ojlem. Xa xe ri ajtz'ib' yetikïr nisik'ij ri rutz'aqikisaxik.

**Taq rutzub'al awachib'al q'equ'm/saq** — Tacha' chi rij ri rutzub'al awachib'al q'equ'm, saq o tacha' chi rij ri ruk'waxik. Ri rucha'ojil nuk'äm ri' pa junanïk taq q'ij.

## K'ïy taq Ch'ab'äl chuqa' Ochochib'äl

**12+ taq ch'ab'äl** — Konojel taq rutz'alajisaxik UI: Ingles, Espanol, Chin, Tagalog, Vietnam, Arab, Frances, Haitiano Creole, Coreano, Ruso, Hindi, Portugues chuqa' Aleman. Ruk'ojlemal RTL chi re ri Arab.

**PWA** — Yetikïr niyak pa jun chike samajib'äl rik'in k'ak' raqän. Ri service worker nuyäk cache ri app shell richin nisamajïk majun k'amaya'l. Tijonel taq rutzijol chi re ri taq riqom.

**Rutzub'al awachib'al ochochib'äl nab'ey** — Rutzub'al awachib'al responsive richin ochochib'äl chuqa' taq komputadora. Ruch'akub'al raqän ri yetikïr nitz'apïx, taq kontrol yetikïr nok chuqa' taq rutzub'al awachib'al yetikïr nijal.

## Rujikib'axik chuqa' Ruk'ulb'al taq Ewanum

**K'ïy taq ruk'ojlem richin ruchajixik ewanum** — Ri awanum ewanin ruma k'a 3 taq ruk'ojlem e k'oje' pa k'ojlemal: jun PIN achike rat xacha', ri awuk'ojlem rucha'ojil chuqa' jun ruchajixik samajib'äl. We xa jun xuk'ulun, man tajin ta.

**Ruch'akunisaxik ri rucha'ojil ruk'ojlem** — Ruk'waxik ruk'ojlem ruma q'ij (achike rat at ajchajinel). Ruk'ulb'al rik'in ruk'axik — majun ya'om rutzub'al awachib'al taq ewanum. Ruch'utinisaxik k'ij chuwäch — Tatz'apij jun samajib'äl xuk'ulun xab'akuchi'.

**Ruyon ruchajixik taq q'ij** — Ri taq q'ij yek'owisaj ruyon pa ri b'ak'tal. Ri ruch'utinisaxik pa q'ij nuchajij ri taq samajib'äl e majun taq winaq. Ri awanum e k'o pa jun ruch'akub'al samaj, majun q'ij nok pa ri ruxaq.

**Ruch'akunisaxik samajib'äl** — Taya' rutzub'al awachib'al k'ak' taq samajib'äl rik'in jeb'ël ruchajixik. Takanoj jun b'itz'ib' QR o taya' jun ko'tz'ib' provisioning. Nisamajïk rik'in ruk'exik ewanum — ri awanum majun q'ij nok toq nik'ëx.

**Taq rucholajel ewanum** — Toq xtatikirisaj, xkatya' jun rucholajel ewanum richin taq q'ijub'. K'o chi natz'aqatisaj jun b'ano' ewanin chuwäch chi natikirisaj.

**Taq ruchajixik samajib'äl** — K'o ruto'onel passkey richin nïm ruchajixik toq xtatikirisaj. Taya' retal jun ruchajixik samajib'äl o biometric, chuwäch chi man tasik'ij taq awetal.

**Forward secrecy chi kijujunal tzijol** — Jun tzijol jun ewanum rik'in jun ketz'ïb' ewanum, chuwäch chi nuya' pa jun ruk'ojlem ECIES chi kijujunal taq winaq eto'on. We xuk'ulun ri ewanum ruk'ojlem, man nitz'et ta ri taq tzijol xe'apon.
